## Part 2 - live probe: all parts ran

### Where reality differs from the documented protocol (most important first)

1. **Tokens live about 4 seconds.** `expires_at` was 4.0 to 4.2 s after minting. An unused token 6 s old was rejected. The browser must fetch the token immediately before `new WebSocket(...)`: never cache or pre-fetch it, and never fetch it before the `getUserMedia` permission prompt.
2. **Auth failures arrive after a successful handshake.** A bad, expired or empty token still gets a 101 upgrade, so `onopen` fires. The failure then comes as `{"type":"error","code":1008,"message":"Invalid or expired token\n..."}` followed about 50 ms later by close code 1008. The browser must handle `type:"error"` and a non-1000 close, not just `onerror`.
3. **"Single use" is not strict.** One fresh token opened a TTS socket and an ASR socket at the same time, and both got `ready`. The same token about 5 s later was rejected, so the limit looks like time, not use count. Keep one token per connection anyway.
4. **The VAD array has four horizons, not three:** 0.5, 1.0, 2.0 and 3.0 s. `vad[2]` is still the 2.0 s horizon.
5. **The VAD reports "inactive" during silence before the user speaks.** With 1 s of leading silence, `vad[2]` sat at 0.76 to 0.90 from 0.18 s until speech began. A naive "`vad[2] &gt; 0.5` means the turn ended" fires immediately. Gate end-of-turn on having received at least one `text` message.
6. **STT text is incremental, not cumulative.** Each `text` message carries one or two new words; the client must append and join with spaces (the teammate's `segments.push` approach is right).
7. **TTS `text` messages are not strictly per word.** The first one carried a whole phrase ("I'm stuck at step five. Give me the instructions", 0.0 to 2.64 s), then single words. For the short sentence there was a single message with the whole sentence.
8. **Extra fields are present on every message:** `client_req_id` everywhere; `stream_id` on audio and text; `audio_tokens` (an integer array) on every TTS audio message, which can be ignored. `ready` also carries `request_id`, `model_name`, `model_ext`, `residency`, the stream name lists, and for STT `delay_in_frames: 10`.
9. **TTS generates faster than real time** (5.12 s of audio in 2.76 s). The browser must schedule playback from a queue, not play chunks on arrival.
